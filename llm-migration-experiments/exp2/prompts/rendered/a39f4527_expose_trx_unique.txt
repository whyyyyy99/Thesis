We can also consider only a subset of columns when determining uniqueness,
controlling which row we keep when duplicates are found:

>>> df.unique(subset="foo", keep="first", maintain_order=True)

│ foo ┆ bar ┆ ham │

│ i64 ┆ str ┆ str │

│ 2   ┆ a   ┆ b   │
│ 3   ┆ a   ┆ b   │
